Academics at Mivas.

Mid Valley school bwera is committed to academic excellence, offering a broad curriculum that equips students with the knowledge and skills required for national and global opportunities.

Curriculum Overview

We follow the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) syllabus at both O-Level and A-Level, with strong emphasis on STEM and humanities subjects.

Subjects Offered

  • Mathematics & English Language
  • Biology, Chemistry, Physics
  • Geography, History, CRE
  • Entrepreneurship, Agriculture, Computer Studies
  • Luganda, Literature in English, Fine Art

Academic Departments

  • Sciences Department
  • Humanities Department
  • Languages Department
  • ICT & Business Department

Teaching and Assessment

We conduct continuous assessments, including tests, projects, and examinations. Learners are prepared for UNEB exams through structured revision and mock exams.

Co-Curricular Support

Academic clubs such as Debate, Science Club, Writers Club, and ICT Club help learners enhance their understanding and critical thinking skills beyond the classroom.

About Us

mid valley school bwera is a community-based government-aided school located in western Uganda. Established in 1998, the school has grown steadily to become one of the leading institutions offering quality secondary education in the region.

Our Mission

To provide holistic, inclusive, and affordable education that empowers learners with knowledge, skills, and values for national development.

Our Vision

To be a center of academic excellence and moral integrity in Uganda and beyond.

Our Core Values

  • Discipline and Integrity
  • Academic Excellence
  • Teamwork and Collaboration
  • Respect and Inclusivity
  • Innovation and Creativity

Facilities

We boast of modern science laboratories, a computer lab with internet access, a library, a sports ground, and boarding facilities to support students' all-round growth.

Admissions

Find out how to apply for admission to our school.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Copy of Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) results slip or previous academic reports
  • Copy of birth certificate or national ID
  • Passport-sized photographs (2 copies)
  • Recommendation letter from the previous school (for continuing students)

Application Procedure

  1. Visit the school or download the admission form from our website.
  2. Fill out the form and attach all required documents.
  3. Submit the application to the admissions office.
  4. Attend the admission interview or orientation (where applicable).
  5. Receive an admission letter upon successful application.

Entry Points

  • S1: Based on PLE performance
  • S5: Based on UCE (O’Level) performance
  • Transfers: Subject to availability and academic merit
